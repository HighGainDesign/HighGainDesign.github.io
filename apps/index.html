Safari extension · Free

Paste Amendment

Paste into websites that block it.

Restore normal pasting in Safari text fields, password fields, and text areas. Your clipboard is never read, saved, or uploaded by the extension.

Get it on the Mac App Store Download the DMG

macOS 13 or later. iPhone and iPad coming soon.

Start pasting in Safari

  1. Open Paste Amendment, then open Safari Settings.
  2. Enable the extension and allow access to your chosen websites.
  3. Refresh the webpage and paste normally with ⌘V or Edit → Paste.
