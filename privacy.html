Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

High Gain Design ("we," "us," or "our") operates consulting services and publishes applications. This policy describes how we handle information across our services and apps.

Applications

Our apps, including LightPad, are designed to respect your privacy:

  • No data collection. Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers.
  • No accounts. Our apps do not require sign-up or login.
  • No third-party services. Our apps do not include advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or any third-party code that collects data.
  • On-device only. All app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not accessible to us.
  • In-App Purchases. Some apps offer optional tip jar purchases processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive any personal or payment information from these transactions.

Consulting Services

When you engage High Gain Design for consulting or advisory services:

  • Minimal information. We only use information you voluntarily provide (such as your name and email) to deliver our services and communicate with you.
  • No sharing. We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties.
  • Expert network engagements. When we work through expert networks, we do not receive client identifying information from the network. If a client shares personal details during a consultation, we do not record, store, or use that information beyond the scope of the engagement.

Data Retention

We retain client contact information only as long as necessary to maintain our business relationship. We do not maintain databases of personal information. You may request deletion of any information you have shared with us at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our apps and services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
